Uniti Privacy Policy

This privacy policy explains how the Uniti mobile app, TV app, and web client handle data. Uniti is designed as a self-hosted media system: your media library stays under the control of the person or organisation that runs the Uniti server.

Last updated: May 4, 2026

Temporary testing notice

This privacy policy is currently published for app testing and release preparation. "Uniti" is used as a working title and does not necessarily represent the final product name, branding, or public release identity.

1. Who publishes Uniti

Uniti is published by Qangaroo IT. For questions about this privacy policy, you can contact Qangaroo IT at info@qangaroo.nl.

2. How Uniti is designed

Uniti is intended to connect to a Uniti server selected, paired, or operated by the user. Media uploads, event data, account data, and library content are processed on that selected Uniti server, not by Qangaroo IT.

Qangaroo IT does not operate a central Uniti cloud for your media library and does not receive your media files, event names, timeline data, or user account contents as part of normal use of the app.

3. Data processed locally on your device or in your browser

To make the app work, Uniti stores a limited amount of information locally on the device or in the browser you use.

  • The selected server address or manually entered server details.
  • Paired device details and pairing tokens needed to keep a paired session working on your own Uniti server.
  • Session cookies issued by your selected Uniti server so the webview can stay signed in.
  • Language, theme, and other interface preferences.
  • Native upload session state, such as queue state, selected target event, and upload progress, while an upload is active or resumable.
  • Temporary local preview files that may be generated on-device for video uploads before they are sent to your Uniti server.

4. Cookies and similar local storage

The web client and mobile webview use functional cookies and similar local storage for technical operation. This includes session cookies from your selected Uniti server and local preferences such as language or theme.

These cookies and local storage entries are used to keep Uniti working properly, for example so you can stay signed in, preserve preferences, and continue using the selected server. They are not used by Qangaroo IT for advertising or cross-site tracking.

5. Data sent to your selected Uniti server

When you use Uniti to browse media, pair a device, sign in, or upload files, the relevant data is sent to the Uniti server you selected or paired with.

  • Sign-in or device-pairing requests.
  • Media files you choose to upload.
  • Upload-related metadata such as filenames, timestamps, dimensions, duration, and generated video preview images when available.
  • Event choices and event details you intentionally create or edit.
  • Requests made by the web client or mobile shell while you browse your library.

6. What Qangaroo IT does not collect from your Uniti library

Based on the current app design, Qangaroo IT does not receive your self-hosted Uniti media library data as part of normal app operation.

  • No central copy of your media files is sent to Qangaroo IT.
  • No central copy of your event library, favorites, timeline ordering, or linked media is sent to Qangaroo IT.
  • No advertising identifiers, analytics SDKs, or third-party marketing trackers are built into the current mobile or TV app.
  • Qangaroo IT does not sell your media library data and does not use it for advertising.

7. Android permissions used by the mobile app

The Android mobile app requests only the permissions needed for its current features.

  • Camera: used to scan QR codes for pairing or sign-in flows when you choose to use that feature.
  • Notifications: used to show native upload progress or completion notifications when uploads continue in the background.
  • Foreground service / data sync: used to keep native uploads running reliably while the app is backgrounded.
  • Internet: used to communicate with the Uniti server you selected.

8. Your role and the server operator role

If you operate your own Uniti server, or if your organisation operates it for you, that server operator is responsible for the media, accounts, retention periods, backups, access rights, and any other processing that happens on that server.

If you use a Uniti server operated by another person or organisation, that operator is responsible for explaining how they process data on their own server.

9. Data retention and deletion

Data stored locally in the app or browser generally remains there until it is replaced, cleared, or the app is removed. Media uploaded through the app is retained on the Uniti server you selected, subject to the settings and management of that server operator.

If you want uploaded media, paired sessions, or other library data to be removed, this must be done on the relevant Uniti server or by clearing the app data from your device.

10. Security

Uniti uses local session state, device pairing, and server-issued cookies to maintain signed-in access. As with any self-hosted system, the security of your media and accounts also depends on the configuration, network exposure, backups, and access controls of the Uniti server you use.

11. Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated when Uniti changes. The latest version will be made available in the app and on the privacy policy page.
